== Terminology
=== Ball Manipulation
Shooting and <<Dribbling Device, dribbling>> is considered as manipulating the ball, the ball accidentally bouncing off the hull is not.

== Tiebreak criteria

The following tiebreak criteria must be applied in the event of a group stage ranking (to determine team qualification order) or when a shootout ends in a stalemate.

* In the group stage: the criteria are cumulative, meaning they are calculated over the entire course of the matches.
* In elimination matches: the criteria are reset, meaning they apply only to that specific match and do not carry over.

The criteria are listed below in order of priority, from most to least significant.

. Most matches points.
    * 3 points for each win.
    * 1 point for each tie.
    * 0 point for each loss.
. Most wins.
. Most goals differential.
. Most goals for.
. Robots perform a kickoff? (0 or 1 point).
. Robots move according to the ball position? (0 or 1 point).
    * E.g. goalkepper movement or defenders forming a barrier at defensive situations.
. Robots respect the Game Controller commands? (0, 1, 2 or 3 points).
    * If the robots respects the Halt command, one point is added (the robots can`t move at the Halt command).
    * If the robots respects the Stop command, one point is added (the robots can`t commit fouls at Stop commands at least half the time).
    * If the robots respects the Force Start command, one point is added (the robots needs to move at the Force Start command).

If the criteria don't break the tie in an elimination match, a coin will be flipped to decide the winner.
